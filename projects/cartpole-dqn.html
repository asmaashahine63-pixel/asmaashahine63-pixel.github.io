Deep Q-Network Agent for CartPole-v1

An applied reinforcement-learning system built in PyTorch that learns a discrete left/right control policy from four continuous CartPole state variables. This case study documents the network architecture, experience replay, target network, epsilon-greedy exploration, and actual supplied 500-episode training records.

01 / Executive overview

Learning to stabilize an unstable system

A cart is pushed left or right to balance a hinged pole. The DQN approximates action values using a fully connected neural network. Interaction transitions are stored in replay memory and minibatches train a policy network against periodically updated target-network estimates. The evaluation objective is to keep the pole upright for as many as 500 simulation steps.

06 / Interpretation & verification

What the results demonstrate

Reward improvement is visible, but stability is incomplete

Average reward increased from 26.48 during episodes 1–50 to 206.14 during episodes 451–500. The highest individual reward was 500, while 41 of the last 100 episodes achieved at least 195 steps. This indicates meaningful progress in the recorded run, but evaluation in a separately loaded model would be required to establish repeatable final-policy performance.

Training-loss interpretation

The MSE curve is an optimization diagnostic, not a direct measure of successful balance. Changes in replay-buffer composition and bootstrapped Q-targets can produce spikes even when reward improves.

Exploration schedule

Epsilon was multiplied by 0.995 after each episode, gradually making greedy actions more common. The planned minimum is 0.01, but the specified 500-episode schedule ends above that floor.

Execution limitation, transparently reported: The supplied package did not include the trained policy_net.pth checkpoint. Gymnasium also cannot be installed in the current execution environment. Consequently this portfolio does not claim to contain authentic rendered CartPole evaluation footage. The four videos show real recorded training metrics. A record_trained_gameplay.py script is included to capture actual gameplay when the required model and environment are available.
