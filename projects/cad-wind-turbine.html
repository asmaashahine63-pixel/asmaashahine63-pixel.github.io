Wind Turbine Blade Design
CAD, CFD, Structural & Modal Analysis

A multidisciplinary investigation of an S834-based horizontal-axis wind turbine blade: airfoil optimization in QBlade, 3D modeling in SolidWorks, steady CFD in ANSYS Fluent, one-way pressure transfer to structural analysis, and modal assessment.

Project overview

The project developed a blade design and evaluated the geometry under a specified wind speed and rotor rotation rate. The goal was to connect aerodynamic loads with mechanical strength and vibration behavior. The figures below are taken directly from the submitted 21-page group report; this page is a documented project showcase, not a new numerical validation.

Design & CAD

The S834 airfoil coordinates were incorporated into QBlade. Nineteen spanwise stations defined chord and twist; SolidWorks loft features produced the 3D blade geometry.

CFD setup & verification

The rotating flow region was meshed with 957,346 cells and evaluated at 9 m/s and 400 rpm using a steady pressure-based k–ω SST formulation. The report notes student-license mesh limitations and unavailable inflation/y+ verification.

Aerodynamic results

Pressure contours show blade loading differences; velocity contours and pathlines visualize acceleration and wake structure. The report gives 19.6 N·m torque, approximately 821 W power and Cp ≈ 0.4 under the selected conditions.

Structural response

CFD surface pressures were transferred to a wooden-blade structural model with a fixed root. Images present imported pressure, equivalent von Mises stress, elastic strain and total deformation; exact margins of safety were not independently validated.

Modal analysis

The report provides six eigenmode shapes and frequencies. The first natural frequency is stated as 155.39 Hz, compared with operating harmonics up to 20 Hz; this only evaluates the modeled conditions and does not prove general resonance immunity.
