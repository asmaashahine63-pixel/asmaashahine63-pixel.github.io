Analog Communications: Modulation, Demodulation & Noise Performance

A MATLAB investigation of conventional amplitude modulation (DSB-LC), suppressed-carrier AM (DSB-SC), phase modulation (PM), and frequency modulation (FM). The project compares time-domain behavior, frequency spectra, signal recovery, bandwidth, and performance under additive white Gaussian noise.

PROJECT APPROACH

From message signal to receiver performance

A two-tone message containing 200 Hz and 400 Hz components is used to study conventional AM and coherent DSB-SC reception. The analysis then extends to PM and FM at different sensitivity settings, before testing recovered waveforms at input SNR levels of 20, 10 and 5 dB.
