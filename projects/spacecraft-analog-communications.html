PROJECT INTRODUCTION

What this project investigates

The study starts with a two-tone baseband message at 200 Hz and 400 Hz, then investigates DSB-LC envelope detection, DSB-SC coherent demodulation, frequency and phase modulation, and signal recovery at 5, 10, and 20 dB input SNR. Each stage is documented using original MATLAB waveforms, spectra, console outputs and recovery plots.

Evidence note: Figures and numerical claims originate from the submitted report and scripts; MATLAB has not been rerun here. Reported noise rankings are specific to the implemented receiver models and random-noise realizations.
